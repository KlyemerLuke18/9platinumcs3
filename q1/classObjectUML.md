## Properties
Property       Data Type        Description

yearCreation   integer          The year of creation of the car
carValue       integer          How much the car costs
performance    string           The overall performance of the car

## Methods
Method         Description:
drive()        If the car allows driving.
capacity()     The capacity of what the car can handle.
maxSpeed()     How fast the car can go.
